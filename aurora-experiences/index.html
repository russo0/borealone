Tour details

Everything you need to know before the evening.

Clear expectations make for a better private experience.

How private Aurora Chasing works +

The chase is entirely private for your party. The start time is decided on the day, normally between 16:00 and 20:00, based on darkness, cloud cover, and Aurora activity. We use the latest forecast to choose the most suitable route. Only one private departure is offered each night.

What is included +

Each Överkalix tour includes pickup from Yellow Aurora House or accommodation within 10 km of central Överkalix and guided driving during the tour. Professional Aurora photos are included. When conditions allow, we photograph your group beneath the Aurora and deliver a carefully edited selection in a private gallery within 72 hours. The final selection varies with conditions, time on location, and group size. Food, drinks, and winter clothing are not included.

Departure locations +

The listed prices apply to departures from Överkalix. Selected Kiruna dates are available through partner arrangements. Vehicle, meeting point, group size, and price are confirmed separately for each Kiruna booking.

What to bring +

Dress in warm layers and bring gloves, a hat, and insulated winter boots suitable for standing outdoors. You are welcome to bring water or a snack if you wish.

Aurora, weather, and cancellations +

The Aurora needs darkness, clear skies, and solar activity. If the forecast does not show a reasonable, safe opportunity, we cancel before departure rather than run a poor night. When possible, we offer the next suitable date during your stay, subject to availability.

Its strength, colour, duration, and visibility can never be guaranteed. If a tour takes place, the full agreed price applies. There are no partial reductions based on the Aurora's appearance or visibility.
