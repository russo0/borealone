Arrival Guide

A quick guide for a smooth self check‑in.

Before you arrive

Your personal six‑digit door code will be sent shortly before check‑in.

How to unlock the front door

1. Find the keypad beside the main entrance.

The keypad is outside, beside the front door.

2. Enter your six‑digit code. The lock unlocks automatically — no “enter” button.

3. Wait for the lock to turn, then press the handle down and open.

How to lock the door when leaving

Close the door fully. Then press the back arrow ⬅ on the keypad.

Locking or unlocking from inside

Press the centre of the round knob above the handle.

Press the centre of the round knob — not the handle.

Need help?

If the keypad does not work, message us through Booking.com.
