The Yellow Aurora House – Arrival & Door Guide

A quick guide for a smooth self check‑in.

Before you arrive

Your personal six‑digit door code will be sent shortly before check‑in. Please keep it private and do not share it with anyone outside your group.

How to unlock the front door

1. Find the keypad

It is mounted on the wall to the right of the main entrance door.

2. Enter your six‑digit code

Type the six digits on the keypad. The lock will unlock automatically after the correct code is entered — there is no separate “enter” button to press.

If you make a mistake, use the arrow button to delete and start again.

3. Open the door

Wait a moment for the lock to turn, then press the door handle down and open the door.

How to lock the door when leaving

Close the door fully first. Then press the back arrow ☒ on the outside keypad. Wait until you hear the lock turn before leaving.

Please always check that the door is properly closed and locked when you go out.

Locking or unlocking from inside

Press the centre of the large round lock knob above the door handle. The lock will turn automatically. This works for both locking and unlocking.

Need help?

If the keypad does not work, please send us a message through Booking.com. We will help as soon as possible.
