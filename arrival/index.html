Arrival & door guide

Everything you need for a smooth, self check-in.

⌁
Your door code

Your personal six-digit code will be sent separately through Booking.com before check-in. Please keep it private.

SELF CHECK-IN

How to unlock the front door

01

Find the keypad

It is mounted on the wall to the right of the main entrance door.

02

Enter your six-digit code

Type the six digits on the keypad. The lock will unlock automatically after the correct code is entered.

Made a mistake? Use the arrow button to delete and start again.

03

Open the door

Wait a moment for the lock to turn, then press the door handle down and open the door.

WHEN YOU GO OUT

Locking the door

Close the door fully. Then press the back arrow ◀ on the keypad and wait until you hear the lock turn.

Please make sure the door is properly closed and locked before you leave.

INSIDE THE HOUSE

Locking or unlocking from inside

Press the centre of the large round lock knob above the door handle. The lock turns automatically for both locking and unlocking.

Need help?

If the keypad does not work, please message us through Booking.com. We will help as soon as possible.
