Arrival – Yellow Aurora House

Everything you need for a smooth and easy arrival.

Welcome! Here is everything you need to arrive comfortably at Yellow Aurora House.

Finding the house

Drive on the main road E10. Keep following it until you see a yellow house with a black roof on your right. In winter, everything looks white — the yellow color helps you spot the house easily.

Parking & Garage

You may park directly in front of the house. The covered garage can be used during your stay. The garage remote is hanging inside the house, between the entrance door and the window.

Entrance keypad

1. Find the keypad. It is mounted on the wall to the right of the entrance door.

2. Enter your six‑digit code slowly. The lock will unlock automatically when the correct code is entered — there is no “enter” button.

3. If you type a wrong number: Press the arrow ◀ to delete the last digit.

4. When the lock unlocks: You will hear a click. Press the handle down and open the door.

Locking or unlocking from inside

To lock or unlock from inside, simply press the centre of the round knob above the door handle. The lock will turn automatically. No twisting needed — just press.

🕒Check-in time

Check-in is available from 16:00. If you need an earlier arrival, please contact us.

🔒How to lock the door when leaving

1. Close the door completely. Push it until it is fully shut.

2. Press the arrow ◀ on the keypad. This tells the lock to close.

3. Wait for the lock to turn. You will hear a click.

4. Pull the handle gently — if it does not move, the door is locked.
