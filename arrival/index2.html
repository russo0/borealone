Arrival – Yellow Aurora House

Everything you need for a smooth and easy arrival.

Welcome! Here is everything you need to arrive comfortably at Yellow Aurora House.

Finding the house

Follow the main road E10 until Gyljen, 200m after the speed trap. The area is quiet and easy to find, even in winter.

Parking & Garage

You may park directly in front of the house. The covered garage can be used during your stay, just like described in your arrival instructions.

Entrance keypad

Enter your six‑digit code. The lock will unlock automatically after the correct code is entered — there is no “enter” button.
If you make a mistake, use the arrow button ◀ to delete and start again.

How to unlock the door

Press the centre of the large round lock knob above the door handle. The lock will turn automatically. This works for both locking and unlocking.

🕒Check-in time

Check-in is available from 16:00. If you need an earlier arrival, please contact us.

🏡Inside the house

Heating, internet, and lighting are automatic. The house will be warm when you arrive.

🔒How to lock the door when leaving

Close the door fully first. Then press the back arrow ◀ on the outside keypad. Wait until you hear the lock turn before leaving.

💬Need help?

If anything is unclear or you need assistance, feel free to message us through Booking.com or your preferred channel.
