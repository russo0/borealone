Arrival – Yellow Aurora House

Everything you need for a smooth and easy arrival.

Welcome! Here is everything you need to arrive comfortably at Yellow Aurora House.

Finding the house

Follow the main road until you reach the yellow house with the black roof. The area is quiet and easy to find, even in winter.

Parking & Garage

You may park directly in front of the house. The garage can be used during your stay, just like described in your arrival instructions.

Entrance keypad

Enter your code on the keypad. After typing the digits, press the back arrow ◀ to confirm. The lock will turn automatically.

How to unlock the door

Press the centre of the round lock knob above the handle. The lock will turn automatically. This works for both locking and unlocking.

🕒Check-in time

Check-in is available from 15:00. If you need an earlier arrival, please contact us.

🏡Inside the house

Heating, internet, and lighting are automatic. The house will be warm when you arrive.

💬Need help?

If anything is unclear or you need assistance, feel free to message us through Booking.com or your preferred channel.
