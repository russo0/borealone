Arrival – Yellow Aurora House

Everything you need for a smooth and easy arrival.

Welcome! Here is everything you need to arrive comfortably at Yellow Aurora House.

Finding the house

The Yellow Aurora House is located in a quiet area surrounded by nature. Follow the main road until you see the yellow cabin with the blue roof.

Parking

You may park directly in front of the house. The area is private and safe.

Entrance

The entrance door is equipped with a smart lock. Instructions for unlocking are below.

🔓How to unlock the door

Press the centre of the large round lock knob above the door handle. The lock will turn automatically. This works for both locking and unlocking.

🕒Check-in time

Check-in is available from 15:00. If you need an earlier arrival, please contact us.

🏡Inside the house

Heating, internet, and lighting are all automatic. The house will be warm when you arrive.

💬Need help?

If anything is unclear or you need assistance, feel free to message us through Booking.com or your preferred channel.
