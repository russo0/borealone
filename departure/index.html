Checkout – The Yellow Aurora House

Clear instructions to make your departure smooth and easy.

Hello! We hope you enjoyed your stay at The Yellow Aurora House. Here is a clear checkout guide to help you leave with peace of mind.

🕒Checkout time

Checkout is by 12:00 PM. If you need a different time, please contact us in advance – we’ll do our best depending on cleaning schedules.

🧹Before you leave – quick checklist

  • • Turn off all lights.
  • • Close all windows.
  • • Place used towels in the Laundryroom
  • • Leave beds unmade – our cleaning team will take care of them.
  • • Check you haven’t forgotten chargers, clothes or personal items.
  • • Dispose of trash in the kitchen bin.

🔑Keys and access devices

If you used any of the following items, please return them before leaving:

  • • Garage remote – hang it back on the hook between the entrance door and the window.
  • • Return the remote to its hook. ⚠️ CRITICAL: Lost or unreturned remotes will incur a replacement fee of 1000,00 SEK ⚠️
  • • Any physical keys (if provided) – leave them on the kitchen counter.

🚗If you used the garage

  • • Make sure the garage door is fully closed.
  • • Return the remote to its hook.
  • • Return the remote to its hook. ⚠️ CRITICAL: Lost or unreturned remotes will incur a replacement fee of 1000,00 SEK ⚠️
  • • Check that no personal items are left inside.

🛜Internet and devices

There is no need to unplug or turn off any devices – the system resets automatically.

🚪How to lock the house when leaving

After completing your checkout checklist and stepping outside, please follow these steps to lock the door:

  1. 1. Make sure the door is fully closed.
  2. 2. Press the back arrow ◀ on the outside keypad.
  3. 3. Wait a moment until you hear the lock turn.
  4. 4. Gently pull the handle to confirm the door is locked.

📸Before you go

If you enjoyed your stay, a quick review helps us a lot and improves the experience for future guests.

If anything is unclear or you need assistance, feel free to contact us through your reservation platform or your preferred messaging channel.
